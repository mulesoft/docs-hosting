Validate a CloudHub 2.0 app after deployment by switching traffic in stages, monitoring health and rollback thresholds, updating DNS, and completing the cutover.

The process has two phases.

* xref:ch2-migration-validate-switch.adoc[] — Identify your traffic source, shift traffic in stages, and watch for rollback conditions.
* xref:ch2-migration-validate-cutover.adoc[] — Verify the migration, update DNS, fail over the DLB, and decommission the CloudHub application.

== Contact Support for Migration Issues

If you encounter issues during or after traffic switching, gather the following information before contacting https://help.mulesoft.com/s/support[MuleSoft Support].

* Org ID, environment name, and control plane/region (e.g., `usa-e1`, `deu-c1.n`).
* CloudHub 1.0 application name and CloudHub 2.0 deployment ID.
* Private space name and ID.
* Timestamps with timezone of when the issue occurred.
* `x-request-id` header values from failed requests (if applicable).
* Replica status and reason (from the CloudHub 2.0 application's *Deployments* tab).
* DLB name and failover status.
* Traffic switch percentage at the time the issue occurred.

Include this information in your support case to help engineering teams diagnose network, routing, and application state issues quickly.
