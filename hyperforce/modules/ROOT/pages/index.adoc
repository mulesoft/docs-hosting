= Salesforce Hyperforce Overview
:page-aliases: deploy-mule-apps-hyperforce.adoc

Salesforce Hyperforce (Hyperforce) provides a modern infrastructure that lets you deploy MuleSoft applications globally with control over data residency and compliance. Deploy your Mule apps rapidly and reliably to locations worldwide while benefiting from enhanced security, scalability, and privacy standards. Hyperforce offers a globally distributed platform that ensures optimal performance for your integrations and APIs.

Some key benefits:

* Data Residency +
Enables you to serve your users globally while maintaining control over data residency and compliance.

* Scalability +
Enables you to grow and scale your business more flexibly and sustainably.

* Security +
Follows least-privileged control, zero-trust principles, and encrypts data both at rest and in transit.

* Agility +
Minimizes downtime for releases and maintenance. Additionally, it accelerates development and testing environments and offers extensive interoperability with AWS, streamlining your workflow.

* Privacy +
Provides you privacy standards that give you control and transparency over your users' data.

* Available: Feature is currently available on Hyperforce.
* Planned: Feature is scheduled for a future release on Hyperforce.
