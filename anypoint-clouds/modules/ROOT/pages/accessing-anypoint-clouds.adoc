= Accessing Anypoint Clouds
:page-aliases: eu-control-plane::platform-access-eu.adoc, eu-control-plane::studio-platform-access-eu.adoc

Sign in to the Anypoint Cloud that holds your organization. Anypoint Platform accounts, organizations, and assets are not shared across clouds. If you have an account in one cloud, create a new account in every other cloud you use.

== Sign In or Sign Up

. Go to the platform URL for your cloud:
+
[%header%autowidth.spread]
|===
| Cloud | Platform URL

| US Cloud (default)
| https://anypoint.mulesoft.com[`anypoint.mulesoft.com`]

| EU Cloud
| https://eu1.anypoint.mulesoft.com[`eu1.anypoint.mulesoft.com`]

| Canada Cloud
| https://ca1.platform.mulesoft.com[`ca1.platform.mulesoft.com`]

| Japan Cloud
| https://jp1.platform.mulesoft.com[`jp1.platform.mulesoft.com`]

| India Cloud
| https://in1.platform.mulesoft.com[`in1.platform.mulesoft.com`]

| Australia Cloud
| https://au1.platform.mulesoft.com[`au1.platform.mulesoft.com`]
|===

. If you do not have an account in that cloud, click *Sign Up* and create one.
. Enter your username and password, then click *Sign In*.

To point Anypoint Studio or Anypoint Code Builder at a non-US cloud, see those products' control plane settings. To point Mule at a non-US cloud, see xref:configuring-mule-anypoint-clouds.adoc[Configuring Mule to Access Anypoint Clouds].
